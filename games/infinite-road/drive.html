Infinite Road

Drive the endless road as far as you can. The road climbs, curves and banks. Nothing interrupts the drive.

Controls

  • W or Up: accelerate
  • S or Down: brake and reverse
  • A and D or Left and Right: steer
  • Space: handbrake
  • R: restart, C: camera, M: mute, Esc: settings
  • T: time of day, Y: rain
  • Gamepad: left stick steers, RT accelerates, LT brakes

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons.

Car model: Kenney Car Kit (CC0). Built with three.js and cannon-es (MIT).
