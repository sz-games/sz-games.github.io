Infinite Road Game

Drive a 3D car down an endless road. The road curves, climbs and banks, the engine follows your speed, and nothing interrupts the drive. Collect coins and push your distance as far as you can.

Play the game

How to play

  1. Press W or Up to accelerate, S or Down to brake and reverse.
  2. Press A or D, or Left and Right, to steer. Steering tightens up as you slow down.
  3. Press Space for the handbrake. It loosens the rear wheels so the car rotates into a corner.
  4. Collect the gold coins to raise your score.
  5. Press R to restart the run instantly, C to switch camera and Esc for the settings.

On a phone or tablet, the game shows on-screen buttons for gas, brake, handbrake and steering. A gamepad works too: left stick to steer, triggers for gas and brake.

About the game

Infinite Road Game is a free browser game. It uses the three.js library and needs no download or account. The road is generated as you drive, so it never ends and never repeats. Low, Medium and High quality settings are in the game, and the game picks one for you if you do not choose.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?
Yes. The game is free to play and has no account or sign-up.
Does the game work on phones?
Yes. On a phone or tablet, hold the on-screen buttons to drive, steer and brake.
Does the game save my score?
No. The distance and the score reset when you reload the page or press R.
Which keys do I use?
W or Up accelerates, S or Down brakes, A and D or Left and Right steer, Space is the handbrake, R restarts, C changes the camera, M mutes and Esc opens the settings.
