Infinite Road Game

Drive a 3D car down an endless road. Collect coins, keep the car between the road edges and push your distance as far as you can.

Play the game

How to play

  1. Press W or Up to accelerate.
  2. Press A or D, or Left and Right, to steer.
  3. Press S or Down to brake.
  4. Collect the gold coins to raise your score.
  5. Keep the car on the road. The run ends when a wheel leaves the road.

On a phone or tablet, the game shows on-screen buttons for gas, brake and steering.

About the game

Infinite Road Game is a free browser game. It uses the three.js library and needs no download or account. The road, the coins and the distance counter never stop while the car is moving.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?
Yes. The game is free to play and has no account or sign-up.
Does the game work on phones?
Yes. On a phone or tablet, hold the on-screen buttons to drive, steer and brake.
Does the game save my score?
No. The score resets when you reload the page.
Which keys do I use?
W or Up accelerates, S or Down brakes, and A and D or Left and Right steer.
